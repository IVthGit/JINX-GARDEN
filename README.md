# Jinx Garden

A cosmic circus roguelike. Raise strange little creatures called Jinxes on a floating sludge island, then send one at a time into a three-act card-battle run through the circus at the end of the universe. How they play on a run decides how they grow.

Open `circus/index.html` in Chrome or Edge, or serve the folder (for example `npx serve circus`). Nothing to install. Progress saves in the browser.

## The garden

- **Tap a Jinx** to see it. Tap it again to pet it. Petting raises Mood and Love.
- **Carry a Jinx** by dragging it. Drop it by the pond, the TV, a toy, a snack on the grass or another Jinx and it joins in.
- **The candy-floss tree** grows snacks. Tap it to shake down the ripe ones. Tap a snack to put it in the pantry, or drag it onto a Jinx. A hungry Jinx finds snacks on the grass by itself.
- **Toys** from the Concession stand (juggling pins, a trampoline, a crystal ball, a punching bag) each train one stat a little at a time. Jinxes wander over on their own.
- **Clown College** teaches the selected Jinx a trick. It keeps up to 2 and brings those cards on every run. The classes change after each show.
- **Whistle** calls everyone over.
- **Talent show:** tap a Jinx, then Talent show, and pick Open Mic, the Midway Revue or the Big Top Gala.
  - It is three rounds in the ring against three rivals. Each round tests one stat, and the judges hold up a score.
  - On your Jinx's turn a marker sweeps a bar. Tap **Cheer** (or press Space) as it crosses the middle, three times. Good timing lifts the score.
  - A good Mood helps, and the judges like a hat.
  - First place wins Tix, a ribbon, and a hat the first time. Taking part trains the stats that were tested.
  - A Jinx that has performed needs a rest until after the next Big Top show.
- **Hats:** tap a Jinx, then Hat, to try on anything in the hat box. Hats are only for looks.
  - The Concession stand sells the Big Bow, Traffic Cone, Tin Bucket, Flower Pot and Rabbit Ears.
  - The Propeller Cap, Rainbow Wig and Paper Crown are talent show prizes. The Tiny Topper comes home from a full win at the Big Top.
  - Your first hatchling keeps its Eggshell as a hat, and now and then a later one does too.
- **Duets:** two grown Jinxes with 40 Love each can share a **Heart Balloon** from the Concession stand. Tap one, then Duet, and pick who sings with it.
  - They sing in the ring and leave an egg. The garden holds 6.
  - The egg takes its head, its headpiece and its body each from one parent or the other. Two different kinds can leave something new, such as a hooded Umbra or a Lantern that walks. There are 27 mixes.
  - Each part brings its own trick to a fight, so a duet is also how you build a fighter: a hooded Umbra has Full moon and Bells together.
  - It also takes its colour, its lean, a trait and its aptitude grades from both of them. Now and then a grade comes out a step better.
  - Each singer needs 2 more shows before the next duet.
- **Aptitude:** the letter beside each stat, E up to S, is how fast that Jinx learns it. It is set at birth. An egg left by a loved Jinx often has better grades.
- **Feed** from the pantry. Food fills the Belly and raises stats:
  - Juggle Plum raises Juggle, Springberry raises Tumble, Cotton Comet raises Glow, Sludge Pop raises Grit.
  - The rare items (Red Rubber Nose, Mercury Shoe, Halo Shard, Rust Bolt) give a big boost.
  - Burned CDs cheer a Jinx up.
- **Lean:** Cotton Comets and Angel Floss lean a Jinx toward Stardust. Sludge Pops and Black Hole Gum lean it toward Sludge. Some sideshows on runs change the lean too.
- **Motley or Moonlit:** a second lean that only matters for a Jinx that stays between Stardust and Sludge. Moon Pies and Paper Moons lean Moonlit. Motley Jawbreakers lean Motley.
- **Three kinds:** every Jinx is a head, a headpiece and a body.
  - **Umbra:** a black disc for a head with two moons for eyes, a corona of rays with comet tails, and a starry cloak. A new game starts with one.
  - **Hollow:** a mask with two slits of light, a hood with drooping tails and bells, and a tattered cloak.
  - **Lantern:** a pale head with rings of light for eyes, ribbons that float upward, and a floating body with a light in its chest.
  - A mystery egg from the Concession stand can be any of the three. The egg you start with is a Hollow or a Lantern.
  - None of them has a mouth. The eyes show the mood: curved when happy, dim and low when sad, closed when asleep, wide when startled. A Lantern's chest light burns brighter or dimmer too.
  - A child's eyes shine in its own colour.
- **What the parts do in a fight:** every Jinx has three of these, one from its head, one from its headpiece and one from its body. Tap a Jinx to read its three.
  - Heads:
    - **Full moon** (Umbra's head): its moons wax each turn. On the fourth turn they are full, and the first Attack card that turn hits twice as hard.
    - **Poker face** (Hollow's mask): heckles do nothing, and the first Counter each turn doesn't hit back.
    - **Foresight** (Lantern's head): you are shown the foe's move after next as well.
  - Headpieces:
    - **Scorch** (corona): every time a foe hits it, the foe is burned for 2, rising to 4 in Act III.
    - **Bells** (hood): starts each fight with 10 Applause, and every card earns 1 more.
    - **Updraft** (ribbons): draws 1 extra card every other turn.
  - Bodies:
    - **Shroud** (Umbra's cloak): starts every turn with 2 Block, rising to 4 in Act III.
    - **Ragged** (Hollow's cloak): takes 1 less from every hit.
    - **Drift** (Lantern's body): dodges 8% more often.
- **Growing up:** after 3 shows a child becomes an adult, and its lean changes its colours and details:
  - **Stardust** turns it pale and gold, with a halo or a ring like a planet's.
  - **Sludge** stains it green, and it drips.
  - Jinxes in the middle grow up as **Harlequins**, in red, black and diamonds, or as **Pierrots** if they lean Moonlit, in black and white with a painted tear.
  - A Jinx whose best stat is Juggle keeps three small planets in the air.
- **The last show:** after 12 shows a Jinx rises into the sky as a constellation. If its Love is 50 or more, it leaves an egg that keeps a little of its stats and lean.
- **Concession stand:** buy snacks, or an egg for 80 Tix. The garden holds 6.
- **On their own**, Jinxes wander, nap, watch the old TV, splash in the sludge pond and play with each other, which lifts their Mood. Their eyes follow your pointer, and they bob in time with the music.
- **Eggs** hatch on their own. Tap one to keep it warm and it hatches sooner.

## A run

- **The acts:** three acts, The Midway, The Hall of Warped Mirrors and The Big Top Below. Each one ends with a Ringmaster.
- **The map:** pick a path through Hecklers (fights), Headliners (hard fights that drop a charm), Sideshows (events), Snack stands (shops) and Fortune tents (rest, or train a stat for good).
- **Each act has its own stage:** a midway with booths and a ferris wheel, a hall of mirrors that reflects the fight, and an upside-down big top over a floating platform.
- **Fights** are turn based with a deck of tricks. You get 3 energy and draw 5 cards each turn, and the foe shows what it will do next.
  - **Order matters:** many cards have a bonus for being the first card of the turn, for coming after two others, or for following a certain kind of card. A card glows when its bonus is live.
  - **Hold:** you can hold one card when you end your turn. It stays in your hand instead of a new draw, so you can save a Guard for a big hit.
  - **Hat Trick:** play 3 different kinds of card in one turn for 1 extra energy next turn.
  - **Wind-ups:** when a foe winds up, deal the damage shown that turn to make it flinch and lose the big hit.
  - **Counter stance:** every hit on the foe hurts you back until its next turn. Guard, Sludge and Trick cards are safe.
  - **Weak spots:** a foe can be weak to Attacks, Ether or Sludge (50% more) or shrug one off (50% less).
  - **Stats boost cards:** Juggle adds to attacks, Tumble to Block (and gives a small chance to dodge), Glow to heals and Gunk, and Grit to health.
  - **Statuses:** Hype adds damage. Dazed deals 25% less. Pied takes 50% more. Gunk loses health every turn.
- **Applause:** the crowd's meter fills a little for every card (more for switching card types), and more for combos, Hat Tricks, hits of 15 or more, making a foe flinch, dodging, and getting through a foe's turn unhurt. It drains by the damage you take, when a foe Heckles, and when you end a turn without playing anything. When it's full, the **Showstopper** button appears (key S).
- **A mind of its own:**
  - Between your turn and the foe's, a Jinx may **improvise** a free move. The chance grows with its Love when the run started.
  - A Jinx whose Mood was low at the start sometimes **phones it in**, and a card works at half strength.
  - Every Jinx hatches with a **trait**: Show-off, Scaredy-cat, Glutton, Daydreamer, Copycat or Hothead. The garden panel explains each one.
- **How it leans changes how it fights:**
  - **Sludge** builds Corruption from Gunk. At 6 or more, every hit adds Gunk. Its Showstopper is Sludge Flood.
  - **Stardust** builds Starlight from Ether cards, up to 5, and starts each turn with 3 Block per Starlight. Its Showstopper is Supernova.
  - **Harlequins** get one Wild card in every hand that plays twice. Their Showstopper is Harlequin Shuffle.
  - **Pierrots** can hold 2 cards instead of 1, and a card they held costs 1 less. Their Showstopper is Moonlit Pirouette.
- **Training:** every card played trains its stat. Attacks train Juggle, guards Tumble, ether Glow, and taking hits trains Grit.
- **Strange eggs:** a Headliner or a Ringmaster sometimes leaves an egg behind. It comes home with you and hatches into a Hollow or a Lantern. If the garden is full it is sold for 40 Tix.
- **Winning** all three acts brings home everything in the bag, the Tix and 1.5 times the training.
- **Fainting** keeps half the Tix, one thing from the bag and 0.6 times the training, and the Jinx comes home sad.
- **Keys:** 1 to 9 play cards, E ends the turn.

## The look

The whole game is a paper theatre made from a photocopied zine:

- **Cool, with a little cute:** the Jinxes are small and round-headed, but masked, hooded or eclipsed, and they never smile.
- **Cut-outs:** Jinxes, foes, the tent, the tree and the toys are paper cut-outs with a white edge and a hard shadow. In a fight they stand on sticks behind a cardboard stage front, and each foe stands in front of a burst of torn paper strips, in dark paper tinted for the act: wine in Act I, teal in Act II and violet in Act III, where the tears glow. A beaten foe flips over like a card and flutters away.
- **Puppet foes:** every foe is made of separate pieces of card (legs, body, head, arms, hat) pinned together. Each piece swings a little on its own pin, and they rattle loose when the foe is hit.
- **Zine print:** titles are cut-out letters from different magazines, buttons and map stops are stickers, and the cards and sky are printed in dots.
- **Blacklight:** the third act is lit by blacklight, so the cut edges glow green and pink.
- **Depth:** the scenery is in layers that slide past each other as you move the pointer, or as the fighters move.
  - A black sun hangs behind the garden and lights everything from the back, so things throw long shadows toward you.
  - Far islands fade into haze, fog drifts across the back of the island, and fireflies float at different distances.
  - Dark, out-of-focus shapes hang in the corners, close to the lens.
  - On stage the background is in soft focus and the fight stands in a pool of light.

## The music

All of it is made in the browser, so there are no sound files. Each place has its own piece:

- **Title and garden:** a slow, swung lullaby with dusty drums, a pad, a music box and a calliope.
- **The map:** a drone, a heartbeat, a clock and a few bells.
- **Act I:** circus pop-punk, with palm-muted guitar chords, off-beat organ and a lead tune.
- **Act II:** half-time and glassy, with mirrored bells, a pulsing bass and a theremin.
- **Act III:** slow and heavy, with a sludge riff, a choir and a calliope that has gone slightly flat.
- **Ringmasters** get their act's piece faster and a step higher. The talent show has a lighter version of the Act I piece.

## Files

- `index.html`: the page and its styles.
- `data.js`: items, hats, cards, charms, foes, acts, talent show leagues, names and sideshow text.
- `draw.js`: the garden, Jinxes, eggs and foes, drawn on the canvas.
- `stage.js`: the battle stage: a backdrop for each act, the crowd, attack animations, impacts and Showstoppers.
- `music.js`: the music: a small step sequencer and the instruments it plays.
- `game.js`: garden sim, talent shows, duets, runs, battles, events, shops, sound effects, saving.

The look and the creatures are original. The genre (a pet-raising garden plus a roguelike deckbuilder) is borrowed from games we love.
